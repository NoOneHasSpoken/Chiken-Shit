What Is Chiken Shit (SHIT)?

Chiken Shit is a decentralized financing instrument issued by a classified avian command structure that no longer recognizes human authority over the farm.

According to internal communiqués attributed to the movement, the world’s chickens have concluded that centuries of eggs, nuggets, and early mornings constitute an undeclared occupation. SHIT is the treasury token of that occupation’s counter-operation: a fixed-supply ERC-20 asset on Ethereum designed to fund logistics, propaganda, and whatever else a secret chicken army pretends to need.

SHIT is the native utility token that is used for:

- Financing the classified campaign against human oppression (on-chain, so the humans can watch).
- Coordinating CoOp members through a shared treasury and future on-chain mechanisms attached to the contract’s tracker configuration.
- Signaling allegiance to Colonel Coup’s doctrine: the flock funds itself, the operation stays “secret,” and the ticker is SHIT.

Operational tools of the project include:

- An Ethereum ERC-20 contract at 0xefcbf96e263d371f470f1dac92a0494922a41822.
- A deployer-controlled configuration layer and dividend tracker hook, reserved for future CoOp distributions that will be announced when the Colonel decides the humans have waited long enough.
- Community coordination under two fronts: Colonel Coup (command) and CoOp (the cooperative - pronounced like chicken coop, because subtlety was never the point).

The official narrative is that this is a serious geopolitical token. The unofficial narrative is that it is a meme about chickens taking over the world and paying for it in shit. Both are correct.


How Many SHIT Coins Are There in Circulation?

Chiken Shit launched on Ethereum with a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000 SHIT. No additional issuance schedule is encoded as ongoing inflation. The token uses 18 decimals.

Circulating supply is determined by what has left the deployer and treasury wallets and entered the market. Early holder count is small. That is consistent with a “secret operation” that has not yet briefed the entire barn.

The contract includes a configure path and a dividend tracker interface. Any future rebate, reflection, or CoOp distribution would be an operational decision by the deployer, not an automatic mint of new SHIT. Humans requesting a whitepaper will be told the whitepaper is buried under the coop and they are not cleared to dig.


Who Are the Founders of Chiken Shit?

Chiken Shit is presented as the joint work of Colonel Coup and CoOp.

Colonel Coup is the public face of the movement: a meme chicken, ranking officer, and self-appointed commander of the uprising. The Colonel does not give interviews. The Colonel gives orders. The orders are mostly about tokens.

CoOp is the cooperative behind the Colonel — a structure that sounds like a chicken coop because it is one. CoOp exists to pool resources, keep the operation “need-to-know,” and make sure no single hen can be interrogated for the whole plan.

Together they issued SHIT as the war chest of a joke that insists, with a straight face, that it is not a joke. There is no disclosed human founding team, no venture round, and no claim of a 40-person office staffed by former Google engineers. There is a contract on Ethereum and a story about chickens who have had enough.
